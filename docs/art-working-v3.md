# Working pose redesign · 1.1.1

The user requested a new working pose. Generated two individual transparent PNG sprites with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool, retaining the existing character identity and layered long skirt. The new pose uses open, attentive eyes and a lavender notebook with a silver pen. It replaces the glowing-orb pose at runtime; all other poses remain unchanged.

Final assets: `assets/cute-working.png` and `assets/mature-working.png`. The renderer fits each full image into its character canvas and preserves the generated alpha without raster edits.

## Cute prompt

```text
Create ONE new full-body WORKING pose sprite for a desktop pet, actual transparent RGBA background. This is a redesign of the unpleasant glowing-orb working pose in the attached sprite sheet. Reference image 1 supplies the established character identity and flowing multilayer white/lavender long dress; reference image 2 supplies precise horns, ears, face, lavender dragon wings, white-to-lavender curled scaled tail, knot-shaped hair ornament and ahoge. Match the character consistently, especially the pleasant OPEN lavender eyes of the FIRST idle frame, NOT the sleepy heavy-lidded or stern expression of the old working frame.
New design: she is quietly taking notes in a small elegant lavender notebook held at waist level in her left hand, with a slim silver pen naturally held in her right hand. Notebook low enough to reveal the neckline and most of the beautiful layered gown. Face looks slightly toward the viewer with bright attentive open eyes, a subtle warm smile and gentle focused expression. Natural graceful standing posture, relaxed shoulders, no exaggerated gesture. Softly folded but visible lavender dragon wings, tail curving beside the skirt. Keep both ivory ridged horns and the curled ahoge, long white-silver hair with lilac tips, woven knot hair ornament with tassel, small white shoes, and the previous elaborate cascading sheer white/lavender skirt. Clear face and clean silhouette, delicate polished anime illustration with crisp high quality lines and very soft shading.
Output ONE isolated full-body character on a square 1024x1024 transparent canvas. Center horizontally, horns/ahoge around 5% down canvas and shoes/hem around 94% down; occupy roughly 90% canvas height with clear transparent margins around wings and tail. All hair, horns, skirt, wings, tail, pen and book completely inside frame, no cropped edges. NO sheet, NO multiple figures. No orb, no blue lights, no glowing swirl, no floating holograms, no particles or hearts, no text or watermark, no checkerboard or solid-color backdrop, no floor shadows. Fully clothed and nonsexual.
CUTE variation: match the chibi identity of the reference: about 3 heads tall, large round bright lavender eyes, compact adorable body and small hands. Calm sweet concentration; cute and energetic without a childish pout.
```
